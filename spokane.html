Good to know

Questions about Spokane

Is Chrome PTC Spokane open 24 hours?

Yes, for members. You can train any time with the GymMaster app over Bluetooth, or a key fob. The gym is staffed 5am to 9pm Monday to Friday and 8am to 8pm on weekends, and staffed hours are subject to change.

How much is a membership at Chrome PTC Spokane?

Month to month is $60 a month with a $50 signup fee and a 30-day cancellation. Prepay options are $174.56 for 3 months, $327.30 for 6 months and $600.05 for 12 months. There are no other associated fees at either gym.

Can I try Spokane before I join?

Yes, with a day pass for $21.82 (18 and older). It is good 5am to 8pm Monday to Friday and 8am to 7pm on weekends. There are no free trials or free guest visits.

Can I use both Spokane and Liberty Lake?

Yes. Add the other facility to your membership for just $30* per month! Ask an associate for more information and to learn how to get started.

How do I join?

Pick your plan above and finish on our secure member portal, or call today to set up a tour and sign up in person. After you join, call or email us to book a short orientation: Bluetooth and key fob setup, and a walk through the facility rules.

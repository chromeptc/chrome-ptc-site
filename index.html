Good to know

Quick answers.

Is the gym really open 24/7?

Yes. Members get in any time with the GymMaster app over Bluetooth, or a key fob. The gym is staffed during posted hours, which are subject to change.

Can I try it before I join?

Yes, with a day pass: $16.36 at Spokane or $21.82 at Liberty Lake. Everyone in the gym is either a member or a day pass holder, so there are no free trials or free guest visits.

Can I use both Spokane and Liberty Lake?

Yes. Add the other facility to your membership for just $30* per month! Ask an associate for more information and to learn how to get started.

What if I want to cancel?

Month-to-month memberships at both gyms have a 30-day cancellation. Prepaid plans (3, 6 and 12 months) renew automatically unless you give notice 7 days before billing. Questions? Call (509) 315-4827.

What happens after I sign up?

Call or email us for a short orientation. We'll give you a tour, set up your Bluetooth and key fob access, and go over the facility rules.

Is it beginner friendly?

Our training is tailored to any fitness level, and our members come from all walks of life: powerlifters, bodybuilders, athletes and everyone in between.
